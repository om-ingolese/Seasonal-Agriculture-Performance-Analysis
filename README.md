\# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis



\## Project Overview



This project analyzes seasonal agricultural performance using a dataset containing information about crops, seasons, environmental conditions, irrigation methods, resource usage, production, market prices, revenue, costs, and profit.



The analysis focuses on identifying patterns in agricultural productivity, seasonal performance, resource efficiency, and economic outcomes.



\## Objectives



\- Analyze agricultural yield across different seasons.

\- Compare crop performance.

\- Examine crop yield across different seasons.

\- Investigate the relationship between rainfall and agricultural yield.

\- Analyze water usage across irrigation methods.

\- Compare water efficiency across irrigation methods.

\- Study relationships between agricultural, environmental, resource, and economic variables.

\- Analyze seasonal profitability.

\- Examine the relationship between production and revenue.

\- Compare market prices across different crops.



\## Dataset



The dataset contains 4,000 agricultural records and 28 variables covering:



\- Crop and season information

\- Farm area

\- Rainfall

\- Temperature

\- Humidity

\- Sunlight

\- Soil characteristics

\- Fertilizer and pesticide usage

\- Yield and production

\- Market price

\- Revenue

\- Total cost

\- Profit

\- Water usage

\- Water efficiency

\## Key Findings



\- Kharif shows the strongest overall agricultural and economic performance among the analyzed seasons.

\- Average yield follows a consistent pattern of Kharif > Rabi > Zaid across the analyzed crops.

\- Sugarcane has substantially higher yield values than the other crops but a much lower average market price per tonne.

\- Rainfall has a very weak linear correlation with agricultural yield in the dataset.

\- Flood irrigation records the highest average water usage.

\- Rainfed farms have the highest average water-efficiency value among the irrigation categories analyzed.

\- Kharif has the highest average profit margin, approximately 25.17%.

\- Rabi has a positive average profit margin of approximately 14.58%.

\- Zaid has a negative average profit margin of approximately -4.78%.

\- Revenue has a strong positive association with profit.

\- Production has a moderate positive relationship with revenue.
